Atomic Blonde (2017) – Frequently Asked Questions

Atomic Blonde is a 2017 action thriller set during the final days of the Cold War. The story follows MI6 agent Lorraine Broughton, who is sent to Berlin to recover a stolen list containing the names of undercover agents. The mission quickly turns into a deadly web of espionage, betrayal, and brutal combat.

Yes. Atomic Blonde is based on the 2012 graphic novel The Coldest City by Antony Johnston and Sam Hart. The film adapts the espionage storyline into a stylish, high-intensity action thriller.

The film stars Charlize Theron as Lorraine Broughton, alongside James McAvoy, Sofia Boutella, John Goodman, and Toby Jones. Charlize Theron performed many of her own stunts, which added to the film’s intense realism.
