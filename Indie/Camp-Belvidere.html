Camp Belvidere : 2024

Description

In Camp Belvidere, Rose, a young and spirited camp leader, and Gin, a reserved and introspective camp nurse, navigate the complexities of their growing affection for each other. Their bond, rooted in shared moments and mutual understanding, blossoms into a passionate love affair. Set in the 1950s, the film poignantly captures the challenges and beauty of a love that defies societal norms.
