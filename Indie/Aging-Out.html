Kasbi : 2024

Description

In a quiet motel room, **Kasbi (2024)** unveils an intimate world where attraction becomes awakening. Maryam, a middle-aged Pakistani housewife in search of identity and emotional release, hires a young, self‑assured sex worker named Aisha for just one night. Their encounter becomes a daring exchange of desire, empathy, and transformation—a short film that challenges cultural taboos and asks: *Is it ever too late to become the person you want to be?*

Cast

Anna Khaja

as Maryam

Zina Louhaichy

as Aisha
