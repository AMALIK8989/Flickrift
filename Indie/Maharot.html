Maharot : 2024

Description

Tantan is a small-town young man, falls for Joni, a free-spirited woman who sells herself yet teaches him about love.
Set against the backdrop of a conservative community, "Maharot" explores the complexities of desire, morality, and self-discovery. As Tantan navigates his feelings and the judgments of those around him, he learns that love can be found in the most unexpected places and people.
This indie drama delves into themes of acceptance, personal growth, and the courage to follow one's heart, even when society disagrees.
