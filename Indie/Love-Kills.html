Love Kills : 2024

Description

Infidelity comes to an abrupt end when the cheating lovers are caught. Lia is in a committed relationship with Milo, but when their new roommate Brianna arrives, an irresistible attraction sparks between them. As secret feelings intensify and forbidden moments unfold behind closed doors, the delicate balance of their living situation becomes increasingly dangerous. When Milo discovers the betrayal, everything unravels. But Brianna's sudden disappearance takes a dark turn, transforming what began as a love triangle into a chilling mystery.
