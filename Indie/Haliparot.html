Haliparot : 2023

Description

Haliparot follows a young woman’s journey through a sexual awakening that unleashes her personal inhibitions. As she explores newfound desires, she finds herself unable to resist temptations that threaten to destroy her family. The film delves into the complexities of self-discovery, the consequences of impulsive choices, and the struggle to balance personal freedom with responsibility. Through its provocative narrative, Haliparot examines themes of temptation, guilt, and the impact of one’s actions on loved ones.
