4.48 : 2014

Description

In 4.48, a young, ambitious actress secures a role that demands her to delve deep into the psyche of a complex character. To embody this role authentically, she immerses herself in method acting, blurring the lines between her identity and that of her character. As she navigates this intense transformation, she faces challenges in her personal relationships and grapples with the psychological toll of her commitment to the craft. The film delves into the complexities of identity, the boundaries between reality and performance, and the emotional resilience required in the pursuit of artistic excellence.
