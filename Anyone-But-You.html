Anyone But You (2023) – Frequently Asked Questions

Anyone But You is a 2023 romantic comedy about Bea and Ben, two people whose perfect first date quickly turns into mutual dislike. When they unexpectedly reunite at a destination wedding in Australia, they pretend to be a couple — leading to tension, chemistry, and plenty of awkward romantic chaos.

The film stars Sydney Sweeney and Glen Powell as the lead characters, supported by a cast that includes Alexandra Shipp, GaTa, Hadley Robinson, and Dermot Mulroney.

Anyone But You is not based on a true story. However, it draws inspiration from classic romantic comedy tropes and loosely references Shakespeare’s “Much Ado About Nothing” in its enemies-to-lovers dynamic.

It is primarily a romantic comedy. The film blends sharp humor, flirtation, misunderstandings, and emotional vulnerability in a modern enemies-to-lovers story.

The movie is rated R for language, sexual content, and brief nudity. It is intended for mature audiences rather than younger viewers.
