Bloomington : 2010

Description

Bloomington" (2010) is a romantic drama film directed by Fernanda Cardoso. The story follows Jackie Kirk, played by Sarah Stouffer, a former child actress who enrolls in college in Bloomington, Indiana, seeking a normal life away from Hollywood. At school, she meets Catherine Stark, a charismatic and alluring psychology professor portrayed by Allison McAtee. As their friendship evolves into a passionate affair, Jackie finds herself caught between her growing feelings for Catherine and the allure of her former acting career. "Bloomington" explores themes of self-discovery, love, and the complexities of relationships, offering a poignant and intimate look at two women navigating personal and professional challenges.
