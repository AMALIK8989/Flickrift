Black Tar Road : 2016

Description

Black Tar Road is a gritty, dark, love story of two women, Heather, a desperate truck stop prostitute, and Charlie, a trucker addicted to heroin. These two lost souls, battle their addictions in hopes of finding love in the middle of nowhere.
