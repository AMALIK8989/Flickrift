Wild Things : 1998

Description

Wild Things" is a neo-noir thriller directed by John McNaughton. The film stars Matt Dillon as Sam Lombardo, a high school guidance counselor in Blue Bay, Florida, who is accused of rape by two students, Kelly Van Ryan (Denise Richards) and Suzie Toller (Neve Campbell). As the case unfolds, it reveals a web of deceit, betrayal, and greed involving unexpected alliances and ulterior motives. Known for its plot twists, steamy scenes, and intricate storyline, "Wild Things" keeps viewers on the edge of their seats until the very end.
