Wild Things 2 : 2004

Description

Wild Things 2" is a direct-to-video sequel directed by Jack Perez. The film follows the story of Brittney Havers (Susan Ward), who becomes embroiled in a conspiracy after her wealthy stepfather dies in a plane crash, leaving her a substantial inheritance. As the investigation into his death deepens, Brittney and her classmate Maya King (Leila Arcieri) become the center of a series of manipulations and double-crosses. The sequel maintains the original film's themes of seduction, deception, and unexpected twists.
