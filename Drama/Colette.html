Colette : 2018

Description

Colette (2018) is a biographical drama about the life of the iconic French author, Sidonie-Gabrielle Colette. In the late 19th century, Colette defies societal expectations to write her own stories, and in the process, she uncovers her true power, challenging her husband and the patriarchal world of publishing. It's a powerful journey of self-discovery, ambition, and liberation.
