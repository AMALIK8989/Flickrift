Home Alone : 1990

Description

Home Alone" (1990) is a holiday family comedy where 8-year-old Kevin McCallister (Macaulay Culkin) is accidentally left home alone during Christmas. With his family away, Kevin must fend off two burglars, Harry and Marv, who attempt to break into his house. Kevin uses his wit and a series of ingenious traps to outsmart the bumbling burglars. Directed by Chris Columbus, this beloved classic is a heartwarming, hilarious, and action-packed holiday adventure.
