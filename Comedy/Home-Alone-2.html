Home Alone : Lost In New York : 1991

Description

Home Alone 2: Lost in New York" (1992) is the hilarious sequel to the holiday classic *Home Alone* (1990). This time, Kevin McCallister (Macaulay Culkin) is separated from his family during their Christmas trip and ends up in New York City. As fate would have it, he runs into the same burglars—Harry and Marv (Joe Pesci and Daniel Stern)—who are now out of prison and planning a new heist. Kevin, ever resourceful, uses his quick wit and a series of clever traps to stop them. Directed by Chris Columbus, *Home Alone 2* is a festive, fun-filled adventure that continues the story of Kevin's misadventures and bravery during the holiday season.
