## How to Contribute

There are many ways to contribute:  

1. **Report bugs or suggest features**  
2. **Improve documentation**  
3. **Fix bugs**  
4. **Add new features**  

We ❤️ contributions of all sizes, from tiny typo fixes to major features.  

---

## Reporting Issues

If you find a bug or have a feature request:

1. Check the [Issues](https://github.com/your-username/flickrift/issues) page to see if it’s already reported.  
2. Open a new issue if it isn’t.  
3. Include as much detail as possible:
   - Steps to reproduce the issue
   - Expected vs. actual behavior
   - Screenshots or logs (if applicable)
