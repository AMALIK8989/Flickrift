Atomic Blonde (2017) – Frequently Asked Questions

Atomic Blonde is a 2017 action thriller set during the final days of the Cold War. The story follows MI6 agent Lorraine Broughton, who is sent to Berlin to recover a stolen list containing the names of undercover agents. The mission quickly turns into a deadly web of espionage, betrayal, and brutal combat.
