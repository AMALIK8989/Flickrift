Mission Impossible 1 : 1996

Description

Mission: Impossible (1996) kickstarted one of Hollywood’s most iconic spy franchises. Tom Cruise stars as Ethan Hunt, a secret agent framed for a deadly embassy explosion. As he races against time to clear his name, betrayals run deep, and no one is who they seem. Packed with edge-of-your-seat action, high-stakes espionage, and the legendary “vault drop” scene, this movie redefined the genre. Now streaming on FlickRift in English with subtitles – experience the beginning of Ethan Hunt’s impossible legacy.
