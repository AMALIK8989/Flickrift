The Babysitter : 2017

Description

The Babysitter' (2017), directed by McG, is a hilarious yet terrifying horror-comedy. When young Cole stays up late to spy on his seemingly sweet babysitter Bee, he discovers she's part of a satanic cult. As Cole fights for his life against the cult members, he must outsmart and survive them. The film blends blood-pumping action with dark humor and a shocking storyline, making for a thrilling ride of suspense and laughs.
